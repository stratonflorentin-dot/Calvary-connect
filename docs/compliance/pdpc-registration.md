# Tanzania PDPA / PDPC data-controller registration

**Status:** ⚠️ Registration action required (to be completed by the company).

Under the Tanzania **Personal Data Protection Act, 2022** and its regulations,
an organisation that processes personal data as a **data controller** (this ERP
holds driver, employee, and customer personal data) must register with the
**Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC)**.

## What must be done (company / legal)

1. Apply for data-controller registration with the PDPC
   (https://www.pdpc.go.tz — registration portal).
2. Pay the prescribed registration fee and obtain the registration certificate.
3. Record the certificate below once issued.

## Registration record

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Registered entity (legal name) | _to be completed_ |
| PDPC registration / certificate number | _to be completed_ |
| Registration date | _to be completed_ |
| Renewal / expiry date | _to be completed_ |
| Data Protection Officer (name, contact) | _to be completed_ |

> This document records the registration obligation and is the single source of
> truth for the certificate once issued. Do **not** enter a placeholder or
> fabricated certificate number — leave fields blank until the real certificate
> is received.

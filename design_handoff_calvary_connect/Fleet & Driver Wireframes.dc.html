2eFinance logic — current vs proposed
Proposal · derived from FINANCE_MODULE_AUDIT_REPORT.md and docs/finance-erp-audit.md

A single-ledger finance logic

The current module is not short of pages — it has 40+. It is short of a single source of truth: money can enter three ways, balances live in mutable columns, and posting is not a privilege. The proposal below changes the logic, not the page count.

Logic today
As audited
Three ways in. Revenue can be created at /income, /finance/income or via invoices; expenses at /expenses, /accountant/expenses and /finance/transactions/expenses. AR can't be trusted because it depends which door was used.
Duplicate masters. accounts (157 rows) and chart_of_accounts (12); three fuel tables; income/sales/revenue; two audit systems.
Ambiguous truth. Journals carry is_posted and status, date and entry_date; invoices carry amount, total_amount and total_payable.
Payment state as columns. Paid-ness sits on the invoice, so partial payments and allocation don't exist. Balances are mutable columns, not views.
Counterparties as free text. client_name / vendor_name strings instead of keys — aging and statements are unreliable.
Currencies summed blindly. TZS, USD, EUR and KES added together on several dashboards despite seeded FX rates.
No period, no privilege. Nothing closes; any authenticated writer can set status='posted' straight from the browser.
Proposed logic
Seven rules
1 · One door per direction. Money in enters only as an invoice; money out only as a bill or an expense claim. The old income pages become read-only registers of what the ledger already holds.
2 · Everything lands in the ledger. No document is "financial" until it posts a balanced journal. accounts is the only chart; the duplicates are retired.
3 · Status is the single truth. draft → posted → reversed. Posted documents are immutable; corrections are auto-generated reversals, never edits.
4 · Payments are their own object. A receipt or payment allocates across invoices, so partial settlement is ordinary. Customer and vendor balances are views over invoices minus allocations.
5 · Every amount carries its currency. Aggregates group by currency and convert at the booking rate. No screen shows a mixed-currency total.
6 · Periods close. A month is open, closed or locked; posting into a closed period is refused, and the close gate lists what still blocks it (see 2d).
7 · Posting is a server privilege. Approve and post run behind an API route with the accountant role, not a browser write. Numbering is issued by the database, so two users can't mint the same invoice number.
